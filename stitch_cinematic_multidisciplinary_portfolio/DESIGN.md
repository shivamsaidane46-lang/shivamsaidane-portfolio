## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an atmospheric, cinematic, and hyper-refined visual environment tailored for a multidisciplinary creative technologist and design director. The brand ethos balances cosmological scale with razor-sharp computational precision. 

The aesthetic synthesizes:
- **Cinematic Void Minimalism:** Uncompromising dark space anchored in deep void blacks, letting creative work act as luminous celestial bodies.
- **Glassmorphic Precision:** Translucent smoked obsidian surfaces, edge-lit diffraction lines, and diffused chromatic radiance.
- **High-Tech Editorial Contrast:** Structural tension between expressive, architectural grotesque display typography and razor-sharp, developer-grade geometric body and data-display type.

The intended emotional resonance is one of elite craft, quiet technological confidence, depth, and deliberate contemplation. Negative space is treated not as emptiness, but as atmospheric distance.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around an uncompromising void base energized by celestial radiance:

- **Base Void (`#020204`, `#05070B`):** Deep, non-reflective foundations that preserve pure contrast and prevent eye fatigue.
- **Surface Layers (`#090D16`, `#0E1424`):** Celestial navy-tinted charcoal tiers for cards, overlays, and structural elements.
- **Primary Energy (`#00E5FF`):** Electric Cyan represents interaction state, hyper-focus, active signals, and primary focal points.
- **Secondary Aura (`#8B5CF6`, `#A855F7`):** Ethereal Violet used for artistic accents, creative taxonomy tags, ambient glow gradients, and subtle visual transitions.
- **Tertiary Resonance (`#3B82F6`):** Deep Satellite Blue bridging the spectrum between cyan and violet for secondary actions and structural indicators.
- **Glass Borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, `rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.2)`): Ultra-fine boundary lines that trace physical geometry without adding visual mass.

## Typography

Typography balances architectural scale with technical precision:

- **Headlines & Display (`Space Grotesk`):** Structural, geometric, and assertive. Tight negative letter-spacing on display tiers (`-0.04em` to `-0.02em`) creates tension and cinematic impact.
- **Body & Longform (`Geist`):** Deliberate, technical, and neutral. Provides absolute legibility against high-contrast backgrounds with generous line-heights (`1.5` - `1.7`).
- **Metadata & Technical Labels (`Geist` in uppercase):** Set with expanded letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to evoke space-age telemetry, console indicators, and curatorial notation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column dynamic grid designed for editorial showcase:

- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns with `2rem` gutters and generous `4rem` outer margins. Content spreads breathe freely with massive vertical breaks (`space-xl` scaled up to `6rem` or `8rem` for major section transitions).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, and `2rem` margins.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, and `1.25rem` margins. Asymmetric alignments collapse gracefully into disciplined vertical stacks.

Negative space must be maintained ruthlessly around hero case studies and interactive demonstrations to maintain cinematic weight.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through optical filtration and multi-layered light emission rather than standard dropshadows:

- **Vignette Background Canvas:** Base viewports utilize a subtle radial gradient falling off into absolute black (`#020204`) at the edges, keeping viewer focus toward central work pieces.
- **Smoked Glass Tier 1 (Base Cards & Modules):** `rgba(9, 13, 22, 0.65)` background combined with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)` perimeter.
- **Smoked Glass Tier 2 (Floating Nav, Modals, Overlays):** `rgba(14, 20, 36, 0.8)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(28px)` and `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- **Cosmic Bloom (Active / Interactive Elevation):** Elevated elements project a diffuse dual-color glow: `0 0 30px -5px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.15), 0 0 60px -10px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.12)`. Borders on interactive elements transition smoothly from white-alpha to electric cyan-alpha on hover.

## Shapes

The design system implements a roundedness tier of `3` (pill-shaped components) to balance brutalist geometry with organic ergonomic forms:

- **Pill Elements:** Interactive actions, filter chips, tags, and status counters use fully rounded pill geometry (`rounded-full` or `9999px`) to create tactile focal islands.
- **Enclosures & Canvases:** Large structural surfaces (showcase cards, media frames, modal panels) utilize disciplined nested radii (`rounded-lg` at `2rem`, `rounded-xl` at `3rem`), softening cosmic visuals into smooth, polished viewing ports.
- **Connective Micro-shapes:** Indicators and toggle elements retain pure circular geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill CTA:** High-contrast solid or neon-rimmed pill. Solid variant uses `#00E5FF` fill with `#020204` text (`font-weight: 600`), yielding maximum luminance against dark surfaces. Glow-rim variant uses `rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.08)` fill, `1px solid #00E5FF`, cyan text, and a focused ambient blur (`0 0 20px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Ghost Pill:** `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` border, transparent fill, crisp off-white text. On hover: border flares to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)` with an ethereal violet underglow.

### Chips & Taxonomy Tags
- Fully rounded pill containers. Smoked glass fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`), ultra-thin border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), uppercase micro-type (`label-sm`).
- Active state transitions the background to `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.15)` with an `#8B5CF6` border and white text.

### Portfolio Showcase Cards
- Obsidian glass container with a subtle vertical light gradient (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(255,255,255,0.06) 0%, rgba(255,255,255,0.01) 100%)`).
- Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Hover behavior: Subtle inward scale of imagery, border shifts to `rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.3)`, and an exterior multi-spectral bloom (`#00E5FF` & `#8B5CF6`) fades in over 400ms ease-out.

### Input Fields
- Recessed dark cavity (`rgba(2, 2, 4, 0.6)`) with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` and pill geometry.
- Text in `body-md` light grey. Focus activates a crisp `#00E5FF` hairline ring with a subtle cyan ambient field (`box-shadow: 0 0 12px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.2)`).

### Selection Controls (Radio & Checkbox)
- Radio: Circular pill shell with a centered `#00E5FF` radiant dot upon activation.
- Checkbox: Rounded square (4px radius) with an electric cyan border and check icon illuminated with a micro-glow.

### Interactive Media Frame / Canvas Viewer
- Bordered viewing module featuring an ultra-fine telemetry HUD (`label-sm` technical metadata tracking project scale, role, year, and coordinates).
- Dynamic cursor-following light refraction across the surface plane.
